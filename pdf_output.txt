Technical Data Sheet: GlassProtect Jeweller
Jeweller
communication technology
Jeweller is a two-way wireless protocol that provides fast and reliable communication between hub and 
connected devices. The protocol delivers instant data-rich alarms: security companies and users know which 
device was triggered, when and where it happened.
Jeweller features encryption and authentication to prevent forgery, and polling to display devices’ status in 
real time. Supporting up to 1,000 m of wireless connectivity, Jeweller is ready to protect facilities and deliver 
the best user experience for both end users and installers.
